OUR STORY
As we believe that Pets, specially dogs; are unique creatures, have beautiful morals, and loyalty, We couldn’t find a better story than that of the famous Japanese Dog “HACHI” to express our passion with pets, to inspire us. So we called our little, baby dream "HACHIKO", to memorize this legendary loyalty Story Between a dog and a human.

Incepted July 2016 with One product made in a room within an apartment, with less than 10 customers, and only two locations to deliver to.. Hachiko now has a portfolio of more than 20 different products, hundreds of customers, a Rich network of distributers including Vets and Pet shops, and a professional home delivery team covers all Great Cairo locations.
