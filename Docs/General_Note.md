Design philosophy
Build the page like an energetic editorial feature: explain the offer quickly, then let people explore at their own pace. Keep the next step clear throughout.
Layout and composition
- Organize the page into distinct chapters: introduction, featured items, how it works, discovery, benefits, FAQs, and gifting.
- Alternate image-led sections with more spacious ones to create rhythm on a long page.
- Pair imagery with short descriptions and scannable labels; repeat the main action at natural decision points.
Interaction and motion
- Make featured content easy to browse with carousels that have clear controls and work well with touch.
- Use accordions to keep answers available without making the page feel crowded.
- Introduce sections and item groups with brief, scroll-triggered motion. Keep it subordinate to the content.
- Support keyboard navigation and reduced-motion preferences; don’t make essential information depend on hover.
Typography and spacing
- Use prominent, expressive headings to signal each section, with highly readable supporting text.
- Keep headings concise, use deliberate line breaks, and give content room to breathe.
- Group related details closely while preserving clear separation between sections.
Responsive guidance
- Preserve the same content hierarchy on narrow screens, prioritizing readable text, imagery, and clear actions.
- Simplify wide-screen browsing interactions for touch and vertical scrolling.
- Adapt navigation and spacing to compact layouts instead of simply shrinking the desktop version.
