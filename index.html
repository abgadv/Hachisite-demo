03 The ritual

From freezer to full-hearted.

Dinner should be the easiest part of the day. Four steps, one microwave, no thawing overnight.

Take a pack out of the freezer. The outer sleeve keeps the inner pack clean and easy to handle.

Slide the sealed inner pack out of the outer pack. That’s the part that goes into the microwave.

Microwave the inner pack. Four and a half minutes sits right in the middle of the range.

Open the pack, let it rest a moment so it isn’t too hot, then serve. Listen for the bowl.

Microwave times vary. Check that the middle is warm, not hot, before serving, and follow your vet’s advice for your pet.

04 Our story

Certified
pet nutrition

We named a company after a dog who waited.

Not because he was famous. Because he kept showing up.

Hachiko, the Japanese dog, waited at Shibuya Station every day for his person. The story is almost a hundred years old and still does the same thing to us: it makes loyalty look simple.

In July 2016, we started with one recipe in a room inside an apartment, fewer than ten customers, and two delivery spots. We called the little dream HACHIKO to keep that story close.

With four years of love, passion, and experience, Hachiko was founded as the first officially certified fresh food company in the Egyptian market. We are a family inspired by passion, and a company directed by science. Today there are more than 20 recipes, hundreds of families, a network of vets and pet shops, and a delivery team that covers Greater Cairo.

08 FAQ

Questions, answered.

The things people ask before the first bowl. If yours isn’t here, the team is one message away.

Message the team ↗
What’s actually in a Hachiko pack?

Human-grade animal protein, olive oil, and — for cats — a balance of four kinds of vegetables. For dogs, the recipe is built around the stage: more protein for puppies, moderate for adults, lighter for seniors, always with the minerals and vitamins they need.

Every 500 g pack is cooked, frozen, and free from artificial flavours, chemicals, and preservatives.

Where do you deliver?

Across Greater Cairo — Cairo and Giza — with no minimum quantity and a flexible time frame. Vets and pet shops are part of the network too.

How long does it keep?

Each pack is good for three months in the freezer. Keep it frozen until mealtime, then follow the four-step ritual on this page.

Can you cook for allergies, diabetes, or other conditions?

Special recipes are available for medical cases such as diabetes, allergies, and disabilities. Tell the team what your vet has advised and what your pet needs; the kitchen can tailor the recipe around it. Hachiko food is not a substitute for veterinary care.

How much should I feed?

That depends on age, weight, and condition — so the team calculates it with you rather than guessing on a bag. They also follow up to help maintain the right weight and body structure. Message them with your pet’s details to get a portion plan.

Can I choose the protein?

Yes. Each recipe has its own protein options: Puppy and Senior come in chicken, beef liver, or a mix; Adult adds fish; and Cats come in chicken, chicken liver, fish, or a mix. Choose one in the bowl builder, and the team will confirm availability for your recipe.

Do you offer gifting?

Yes — fresh dinners wrapped with a note from you. Use the gift note preview above, then message the team to choose the recipes and arrange delivery.
